VEYRA Milestone 12 — Email Notifications

1. Back up backend/app/main.py.
2. Replace backend/app/main.py with the version in this patch.
3. Merge the SMTP variables from backend/.env.example into your existing backend/.env. Do NOT replace your existing .env; preserve DATABASE_URL, ADMIN_API_TOKEN, CUSTOMER_TOKEN_SECRET, and other settings.
4. Keep EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED=false until SMTP details are configured and tested.
5. Restart the backend. Open /docs, use GET /api/v1/admin/notifications/status, then POST /api/v1/admin/notifications/test with your own email. The endpoints require X-Admin-Token containing your current signed-in admin session token.
6. Enable EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED=true only after test delivery succeeds. Never paste SMTP credentials into chat or commit .env.

No frontend files or database schema changes are required for this milestone. Email delivery is best-effort; failed SMTP sends do not roll back orders.
